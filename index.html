About BreakEven Lab

Project Objective

Designed for the Technological Entrepreneurship curriculum, BreakEven Lab provides entrepreneurs and engineering managers an intuitive decision-support tool to calculate financial feasibility, assess risk, and project revenue milestones.

Problem Statement

Many early-stage hardware startups and manufacturing enterprises struggle to accurately quantify fixed and variable overheads. Miscalculating unit economics leads to underpriced products and unmanageable operational losses.

Importance in Mechanical Engineering

In manufacturing environments, break-even analysis guides crucial operational decisions such as process selection, tooling investments, batch sizing, capacity planning, and make-or-buy decisions.

Key Mathematical Formulas

  • Contribution / Unit: $P - V$
  • Break-Even Point (Units): $\frac{FC}{P - V}$
  • Total Revenue: $P \times Q$
  • Total Cost: $FC + (V \times Q)$
